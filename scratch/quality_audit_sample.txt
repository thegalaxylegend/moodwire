[Physics | Q1] Exam: JEEMains | Class: 12 | Topic: Alternating Current | Type: Integer
Question Text:
An AC circuit consists of a resistor \(R = 10\,\Omega\) and an inductor \(L = 0.2\,\text{H}\) connected in series to a sinusoidal source of rms voltage \(V = 100\,\text{V}\) at frequency \(f = 50\,\text{Hz}\). Calculate the power factor of the circuit (expressed as a decimal to two decimal places).
Options:
Correct Answer: 0.16
Explanation:
Step 1: Compute the inductive reactance \(X_L = 2\pi f L = 2\pi \times 50 \times 0.2 = 20\pi \approx 62.83\,\Omega\).\
Step 2: Find the total impedance \(Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} \approx \sqrt{10^2 + 62.83^2} \approx 63.6\,\Omega\).\
Step 3: Power factor \(\cos\phi = R/Z \approx 10/63.6 \approx 0.157\). Rounded to two decimal places gives 0.16.

[Physics | Q3] Exam: JEEMains | Class: 12 | Topic: Physics | Type: MCQ
Question Text:
Which of the following statements about electromagnetic waves propagating in vacuum is correct?
Options:
  A. The electric and magnetic fields are parallel to each other and perpendicular to the direction of propagation.
  B. The magnetic field leads the electric field by $90^{\circ}$.
  C. The energy flux is given by $S = \mathbf{E}\cdot\mathbf{H}$.
  D. The wave speed is $c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}$.
Correct Answer: The wave speed is $c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}$.
Explanation:
In vacuum, $\mathbf{E}$, $\mathbf{B}$, and the propagation direction are mutually perpendicular and in phase. The Poynting vector is $\mathbf{S}=\mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H}$, not a dot product. The correct speed relation is $c=1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}$.

[Chemistry | Q5] Exam: JEEMains | Class: 12 | Topic: Amines and Diazonium Salts | Type: MCQ
Question Text:
Among the following amines, which one exhibits the highest basicity in aqueous solution? Assume that all substituents are alkyl groups and that the solvent is water.
Options:
  A. Aniline (C₆H₅NH₂)
  B. n‑Propylamine (CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂)
  C. Dimethylamine ((CH₃)₂NH)
  D. Triethylamine (N(CH₂CH₃)₃)
Correct Answer: Dimethylamine ((CH₃)₂NH)
Explanation:
Aniline is less basic because the lone pair on nitrogen is delocalised into the aromatic ring (resonance). n‑Propylamine has only one alkyl group, giving a weaker +I effect than dimethylamine. Dimethylamine benefits from two electron‑donating alkyl groups and suffers little steric hindrance, making it the most basic. Triethylamine, despite having three alkyl groups, experiences steric hindrance that reduces its basicity relative to dimethylamine.

[Mathematics | Q2] Exam: JEEMains | Class: 12 | Topic: Inverse Trigonometric Functions | Type: MCQ
Question Text:
Solve the equation $\sin^{-1}(2x) + \cos^{-1}(x) = \frac{3\pi}{2}$ for $x$ in the interval $[-\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}]$.
Options:
  A. No real solution
  B. $x = 0$
  C. $x = \frac{1}{2}$
  D. $x = -\frac{1}{2}$
Correct Answer: No real solution
Explanation:
The domains give $-\frac12\le x\le\frac12$. Using $\sin^{-1}y+\cos^{-1}y=\frac{\pi}{2}$, the equation becomes $\sin^{-1}(2x)-\sin^{-1}(x)=\pi$. The left‑hand side can at most be $\pi$, achieved only when $\sin^{-1}(2x)=\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\sin^{-1}(x)=-\frac{\pi}{2}$, which require $x=\frac12$ and $x=-1$ simultaneously – impossible. Hence no real $x$ satisfies the equation.

Options:
  A. \pi
  B. 2\pi
  C. 4\pi
  D. No finite period
Correct Answer: 2\pi
Explanation:
