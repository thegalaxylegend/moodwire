# 🚀 ExamCompass: Roadmap to 10K Monthly Views

## 1. Content Scaling (The "80/20" Rule)
- **Long-Tail Focus**: Stop targeting "JEE Physics." Target "JEE Physics 2026 Rotational Motion Formulas PDF."
- **Listicles**: Create "Top 10" style posts. e.g., "Top 10 Most Repeated Questions in NEET Biology 2024-2025."
- **Daily Questions**: Use your AI to generate "Question of the Day" posts. These are fast to create and keep people coming back.

## 2. Technical SEO (The Foundation)
- [x] **Cross-Browser Optimization**: Ensure site looks premium on Chrome, Safari, and Edge.
- [x] **Public PDF Downloads**: EXTREMELY IMPORTANT. Let people download for free to get backlinks from educational forums.
- [/] **IndexNow**: Instant notification to Bing/DDG/Yahoo when new questions are added.

## 3. Distribution Strategy
- **YouTube Shorts/Reels**: Make 60-second "Formula Tricks" videos and link to your Topic pages in the bio.
- **WhatsApp Channels**: Create a channel for daily revision notes. Send the "Download PDF" link directly there.
- **Reddit/Quora**: Answer questions on r/JEENEETards or Quora by quoting a small part of your notes and linking to the "Full Solution PDF."

## 4. Backlink Building
- **Guest Posts**: Reach out to small educational blogs.
- **Infographics**: Create "Physics Formula Sheets" as images. When people use them, they'll credit your site.

## Monthly Checklist
1. **Week 1**: Audit SEO keywords using Google Search Console.
2. **Week 2**: Update top 5 performing pages with better internal links.
3. **Week 3**: Publish 4 listicle-style blog posts.
4. **Week 4**: Run a "Mock Test" campaign on social media.
