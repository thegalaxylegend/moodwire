## What is Agriculture?

Agriculture is the practice of cultivating land and breeding plants and animals to provide food, fiber, medicinal plants, and other products to sustain and enhance human life. It includes farming, livestock, and forestry. For Class 11 exam prep in 2026, the most important aspect is understanding the different types of farming and their impact on the economy.


## What is Agriculture?

Agriculture is the backbone of the Indian economy, providing employment to over 50% of the workforce. It includes subsistence farming, commercial farming, and organic farming. The Green Revolution played a significant role in increasing food production in India.


| Type of Farming | Characteristics |
| --- | --- |
| Subsistence Farming | For personal consumption |
| Commercial Farming | For profit |
| Organic Farming | Without synthetic fertilizers and pesticides |


## What are the different types of crops?

Crops can be classified into food crops and non-food crops. Food crops include rice, wheat, and pulses, while non-food crops include cotton, sugarcane, and tobacco.


| Type of Crop | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Food Crops | Rice, Wheat, Pulses |
| Non-Food Crops | Cotton, Sugarcane, Tobacco |

## MCQs


**1. What is the main difference between subsistence farming and commercial farming?**
a) Subsistence farming is done for personal consumption, while commercial farming is done for profit
b) Subsistence farming is done for profit, while commercial farming is done for personal consumption
c) Subsistence farming is done using organic methods, while commercial farming uses synthetic fertilizers
d) Subsistence farming is done in urban areas, while commercial farming is done in rural areas

**Answer:** a) Subsistence farming is done for personal consumption, while commercial farming is done for profit.


**2. Which of the following is a non-food crop?**
a) Rice
b) Wheat
c) Cotton
d) Pulses

**Answer:** c) Cotton is a non-food crop.
