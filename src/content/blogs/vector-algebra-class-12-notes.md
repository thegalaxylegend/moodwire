## ⚡ Formula Bank
- $\vec{a} = \left( a_1, a_2, a_3 \right)$ is a vector with components $a_1, a_2, a_3$
- $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \left| \vec{a} \right| \left| \vec{b} \right| \cos \theta$ is the dot product of two vectors
- $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \left| \vec{a} \right| \left| \vec{b} \right| \sin \theta \hat{n}$ is the cross product of two vectors
- $\left| \vec{a} \right| = \sqrt{a_1^2 + a_2^2 + a_3^2}$ is the magnitude of a vector
- $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = \left| \vec{a} \right|^2$ is the dot product of a vector with itself
- $\vec{a} \times \vec{a} = \vec{0}$ is the cross product of a vector with itself
- $\vec{a} \cdot \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{c} \right) = \vec{b} \cdot \left( \vec{c} \times \vec{a} \right) = \vec{c} \cdot \left( \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \right)$ is the scalar triple product
- $\vec{a} \times \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{c} \right) = \vec{b} \left( \vec{a} \cdot \vec{c} \right) - \vec{c} \left( \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \right)$ is the vector triple product
- $\vec{a} = \lambda \vec{b}$ implies $\vec{a}$ is parallel to $\vec{b}$ if $\lambda$ is a scalar
- $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0$ implies $\vec{a}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{b}$
- $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \vec{0}$ implies $\vec{a}$ is parallel to $\vec{b}$

## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks
- Not checking if vectors are parallel or perpendicular before applying formulas
- Forgetting to calculate the magnitude of vectors before applying formulas
- Not using the correct formula for the cross product of two vectors
- Not using the correct formula for the dot product of two vectors
- Not using the properties of the scalar triple product and vector triple product to simplify calculations

## ✏️ 3 Solved PYQs
- **Question 1:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right)$ and $\vec{b} = \left( 4, 5, 6 \right)$, find $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ and $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$
  - $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \left( 1 \right) \left( 4 \right) + \left( 2 \right) \left( 5 \right) + \left( 3 \right) \left( 6 \right) = 4 + 10 + 18 = 32$
  - $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i} \left( 12 - 15 \right) - \hat{j} \left( 6 - 12 \right) + \hat{k} \left( 5 - 8 \right) = -3 \hat{i} + 6 \hat{j} - 3 \hat{k} = \left( -3, 6, -3 \right)$
- **Question 2:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 1, 0, 0 \right)$, $\vec{b} = \left( 0, 1, 0 \right)$, and $\vec{c} = \left( 0, 0, 1 \right)$, find $\vec{a} \cdot \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{c} \right)$
  - $\vec{b} \times \vec{c} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i} \left( 1 - 0 \right) - \hat{j} \left( 0 - 0 \right) + \hat{k} \left( 0 - 0 \right) = \hat{i} = \left( 1, 0, 0 \right)$
  - $\vec{a} \cdot \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{c} \right) = \left( 1 \right) \left( 1 \right) + \left( 0 \right) \left( 0 \right) + \left( 0 \right) \left( 0 \right) = 1$
- **Question 3:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 2, 3, 4 \right)$ and $\vec{b} = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right)$, find $\vec{a} \times \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{a} \right)$
  - $\vec{b} \times \vec{a} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i} \left( 8 - 9 \right) - \hat{j} \left( 4 - 6 \right) + \hat{k} \left( 3 - 4 \right) = - \hat{i} + 2 \hat{j} - \hat{k} = \left( -1, 2, -1 \right)$
  - $\vec{a} \times \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{a} \right) = \vec{b} \left( \vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} \right) - \vec{a} \left( \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \right) = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right) \left( 4 + 9 + 16 \right) - \left( 2, 3, 4 \right) \left( 2 + 6 + 12 \right) = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right) \left( 29 \right) - \left( 2, 3, 4 \right) \left( 20 \right) = \left( 29, 58, 87 \right) - \left( 40, 60, 80 \right) = \left( -11, -2, 7 \right)$

## 📝 Practice MCQs
**1. Question:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right)$ and $\vec{b} = \left( 4, 5, 6 \right)$, what is $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$?
-
A) 20
-
B) 32
-
C) 40
-
D) 50

**Answer:** B) 32. $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \left( 1 \right) \left( 4 \right) + \left( 2 \right) \left( 5 \right) + \left( 3 \right) \left( 6 \right) = 4 + 10 + 18 = 32$

**2. Question:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 1, 0, 0 \right)$, $\vec{b} = \left( 0, 1, 0 \right)$, and $\vec{c} = \left( 0, 0, 1 \right)$, what is $\vec{a} \cdot \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{c} \right)$?
-
A) 0
-
B) 1
-
C) 2
-
D) 3

**Answer:** B) 1. $\vec{b} \times \vec{c} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i} \left( 1 - 0 \right) - \hat{j} \left( 0 - 0 \right) + \hat{k} \left( 0 - 0 \right) = \hat{i} = \left( 1, 0, 0 \right)$, $\vec{a} \cdot \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{c} \right) = \left( 1 \right) \left( 1 \right) + \left( 0 \right) \left( 0 \right) + \left( 0 \right) \left( 0 \right) = 1$

**3. Question:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 2, 3, 4 \right)$ and $\vec{b} = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right)$, what is $\vec{a} \times \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{a} \right)$?
-
A) $\left( -11, -2, 7 \right)$
-
B) $\left( 11, 2, -7 \right)$
-
C) $\left( -1, 2, -3 \right)$
-
D) $\left( 1, -2, 3 \right)$

**Answer:** A) $\left( -11, -2, 7 \right)$. $\vec{b} \times \vec{a} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i} \left( 8 - 9 \right) - \hat{j} \left( 4 - 6 \right) + \hat{k} \left( 3 - 4 \right) = - \hat{i} + 2 \hat{j} - \hat{k} = \left( -1, 2, -1 \right)$, $\vec{a} \times \left( \vec{b} \times \vec{a} \right) = \vec{b} \left( \vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} \right) - \vec{a} \left( \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \right) = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right) \left( 4 + 9 + 16 \right) - \left( 2, 3, 4 \right) \left( 2 + 6 + 12 \right) = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right) \left( 29 \right) - \left( 2, 3, 4 \right) \left( 20 \right) = \left( 29, 58, 87 \right) - \left( 40, 60, 80 \right) = \left( -11, -2, 7 \right)$

**4. Question:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 1, 2, 3 \right)$, what is $\left| \vec{a} \right|$?
-
A) $\sqrt{14}$
-
B) $\sqrt{28}$
-
C) $\sqrt{14}$
-
D) $\sqrt{38}$

**Answer:** B) $\sqrt{14}$. $\left| \vec{a} \right| = \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 9} = \sqrt{14}$

**5. Question:** If $\vec{a} = \left( 1, 0, 0 \right)$ and $\vec{b} = \left( 0, 1, 0 \right)$, what is $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$?
-
A) $\left( 0, 0, 1 \right)$
-
B) $\left( 0, 0, -1 \right)$
-
C) $\left( 1, 0, 0 \right)$
-
D) $\left( 0, 1, 0 \right)$

**Answer:** A) $\left( 0, 0, 1 \right)$. $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i} \left( 0 - 0 \right) - \hat{j} \left( 0 - 0 \right) + \hat{k} \left( 1 - 0 \right) = \hat{k} = \left( 0, 0, 1 \right)$
