The key factors influencing Indian climate include:
| Factor | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Latitude** | India's location between 8°4' and 37°6' north latitude influences its climate, with the tropics experiencing high temperatures and high levels of precipitation. |
| **Altitude** | The Himalayan mountains and other high-altitude regions experience a temperate climate, while the coastal regions have a tropical climate. |
| **Distance from the Sea** | The distance from the sea affects the climate, with coastal regions experiencing a maritime climate and inland regions experiencing a continental climate. |

## What are the Regional Variations in Indian Climate?
The regional variations in Indian climate are significant, with different parts of the country experiencing different climate conditions. 
- **Tropical Climate**: The southern states of India, such as Tamil Nadu and Kerala, experience a tropical climate, with high temperatures and high levels of precipitation throughout the year.
- **Temperate Climate**: The northern states, such as Jammu and Kashmir, experience a temperate climate, with cold winters and mild summers.
- **Desert Climate**: The northwestern state of Rajasthan experiences a desert climate, with very little precipitation and extreme temperatures.
