## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks

* In the chapter of Kinematics, a common mistake is not considering the sign of the velocity and acceleration while solving problems, leading to incorrect results.
* In the chapter of Work, Energy and Power, students often forget to consider the work done by friction and other non-conservative forces while calculating the total energy of a system.
* In the chapter of Rotational Motion, a trap question is to not consider the direction of the torque and angular momentum while solving problems, leading to incorrect results.
* In the chapter of Gravitation, a common mistake is to not consider the negative sign of the gravitational potential energy, leading to incorrect results.
* In the chapter of Simple Harmonic Motion, students often confuse the equations of motion for simple harmonic motion with those of uniform circular motion, leading to incorrect results.
