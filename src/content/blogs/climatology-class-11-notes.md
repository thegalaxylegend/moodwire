## Core Concepts
The following are the key concepts in climatology:

1. **Temperature**: The degree of heat or cold measured on a **temperature scale**.
2. **Humidity**: The amount of **water vapor** present in the air.
3. **Atmospheric Pressure**: The weight of the atmosphere on the Earth's surface.
4. **Wind**: The movement of air from high to low-pressure areas.
5. **Precipitation**: Water that falls to the Earth's surface, including **rain**, **snow**, **sleet**, and **hail**.

## Climatic Elements
The following are the primary climatic elements:

1. **Temperature**: Measured using a **thermometer**.
2. **Humidity**: Measured using a **hygrometer**.
3. **Cloudiness**: Measured using a **cloud atlas**.
4. **Wind**: Measured using an **anemometer**.
5. **Precipitation**: Measured using a **rain gauge**.

### Trap Exceptions
Be cautious of the following exceptions:

* **Temperature Inversion**: A phenomenon where temperature increases with altitude.
* **Humidity Anomaly**: A situation where humidity is higher or lower than expected.
* **Wind Shear**: A change in wind speed or direction with height.

## Types of Climates
The following are the main types of climates:

1. **Tropical Climate**: Characterized by high temperatures and high humidity.
2. **Desert Climate**: Characterized by low temperatures and low humidity.
3. **Temperate Climate**: Characterized by moderate temperatures and moderate humidity.
4. **Polar Climate**: Characterized by low temperatures and low humidity.

### JEE/NEET Data
For JEE/NEET aspirants, it's essential to remember the following data:

* The **troposphere** is the lowest layer of the atmosphere, extending up to 12 km.
* The **stratosphere** is the second layer of the atmosphere, extending from 12 km to 50 km.
* The **mesosphere** is the third layer of the atmosphere, extending from 50 km to 80 km.

## Climatic Factors
The following are the key factors that influence climate:

1. **Latitude**: The distance from the equator.
2. **Altitude**: The height above sea level.
3. **Ocean Currents**: The movement of water in the oceans.
4. **Landforms**: The shape and features of the land.
