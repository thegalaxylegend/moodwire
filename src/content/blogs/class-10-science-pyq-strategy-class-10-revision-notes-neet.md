## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks

* **Confusing photosynthesis and respiration**: Students often confuse the two processes, forgetting that photosynthesis is the process y which plants produce glucose from carbon dioxide and water, while respiration is the process y which cells break down glucose to produce energy.
* **Forgetting the differences between plant and animal cells**: A common mistake is not being able to distinguish between plant and animal cells, particularly when it comes to the presence or absence of cell walls, chloroplasts, n vacuoles.
* **Mistaking symbiotic relationships for parasitic ones**: Students may struggle to understand the difference between mutualism, commensalism, n parasitism, leading to incorrect identification of relationships between different species.
* **Incorrectly identifying the functions of different parts of a plant**: Students may mistakenly identify the functions of roots, stems, n leaves, or forget the importance of other structures such as flowers, fruits, n seeds.
* **Not understanding the concept of ecosystem balance**: A common mistake is not recognizing the delicate balance within ecosystems and the impact that human activities can have on the environment, leading to incorrect answers about conservation and sustainability.
