## MCQs


**1. What is the main objective of the 73rd Constitutional Amendment?**
a) To empower state governments
b) To strengthen local self-governments
c) To promote economic development
d) To enhance national security

**Answer:** b) The 73rd Constitutional Amendment aims to strengthen local self-governments by giving more powers to panchayats and municipalities.


**2. Which of the following is a function of local government?**
a) Foreign policy
b) National defense
c) Local planning
d) Economic planning

**Answer:** c) Local planning is a function of local government, which includes planning for local infrastructure, services, and development.
