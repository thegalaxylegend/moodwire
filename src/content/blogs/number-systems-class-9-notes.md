## ⚡ Formula Bank
The number system is a fundamental concept in mathematics, and mastering its formulas is crucial for success in Class 9 exams. The following formulas are essential for solving problems related to number systems:
- The **Euclidean algorithm** for finding the greatest common divisor (GCD) of two integers $a$ and $b$ is given by: 
gcd(a, b) = gcd(b, a mod b)
- The **fundamental theorem of arithmetic** states that every integer greater than 1 is either prime itself or can be factorized as a product of prime numbers uniquely, except for the order in which the prime numbers occur.
- To determine if a number is divisible by another number, we use the **divisibility rules**, such as:
  - A number is divisible by 2 if its last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, or 8).
  - A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.
  - A number is divisible by 4 if the number formed by its last two digits is divisible by 4.
- The **the least common multiple (LCM)** of two integers $a$ and $b$ is given by:
	ext{lcm}(a, b) = $\frac{|ab|}${gcd(a, b)}

## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks
When solving problems related to number systems, students often make mistakes that can cost them valuable marks. Here are five common mistakes to avoid:
1. **Not checking for divisibility**: Failing to check if a number is divisible by another number can lead to incorrect solutions.
2. **Incorrectly applying the Euclidean algorithm**: The Euclidean algorithm should be applied correctly to find the GCD of two numbers.
3. **Not considering all possible cases**: Students should consider all possible cases when solving problems related to number systems.
4. **Making calculation errors**: Calculation errors can lead to incorrect solutions, so it's essential to double-check calculations.
5. **Not using the fundamental theorem of arithmetic**: The fundamental theorem of arithmetic should be used to factorize numbers into their prime factors.

## ✏️ 3 Solved PYQs
Here are three solved previous year questions (PYQs) to help students understand how to apply the concepts of number systems:
1. **PYQ 1**: Find the GCD of 48 and 18 using the Euclidean algorithm.
gcd(48, 18) = gcd(18, 48 mod 18) = gcd(18, 12) = gcd(12, 18 mod 12) = gcd(12, 6) = 6
2. **PYQ 2**: Determine if 1234 is divisible by 4.
The last two digits of 1234 are 34, which is not divisible by 4. Therefore, 1234 is not divisible by 4.
3. **PYQ 3**: Find the LCM of 12 and 15.
	ext{lcm}(12, 15) = $\frac{|12 \times 15|}${gcd(12, 15)} = $\frac{180}${3} = 60
