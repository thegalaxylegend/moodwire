## ⚡ Formula Bank
- Area of a circle = πr²
- Circumference of a circle = 2πr
- Area of a sector of a circle = (θ/360) × πr²
- Length of an arc of a circle = (θ/360) × 2πr
- Area of a ring = π(R² - r²)
- Area of a semicircle = (1/2)πr²
- Perimeter of a semicircle = πr + 2r
