## <a id="-formula-bank"></a>⚡ Formula Bank

- **Area of a Circle:** \pi \times {r}^{2} — where $r$ is the radius of the circle, and $\pi$ is a constant approximately equal to 3.14159,- **Circumference of a Circle:** 2 \times \pi \times {r} — where $r$ is the radius of the circle,- **Area of a Sector:** \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi \times {r}^{2} — where $\theta$ is the central angle in degrees, and $r$ is the radius of the circle,- **Length of an Arc:** \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2 \times \pi \times {r} — where $\theta$ is the central angle in degrees, and $r$ is the radius of the circle,- **Area of a Semi-Circle:** \frac{1}{2} \times \pi \times {r}^{2} — where $r$ is the radius of the circle,- **Perimeter of a Semi-Circle:** \pi \times {r} + 2 \times {r} — where $r$ is the radius of the circle,- **Area of a Ring or Annulus:** \pi \times ({R}^{2} - {r}^{2}) — where $R$ is the outer radius and $r$ is the inner radius,- **Area of a Segment:** \frac{1}{2} \times {r}^{2} \times (\theta - \sin{\theta}) — where $\theta$ is the central angle in radians, and $r$ is the radius of the circle,- **Length of a Chord:** 2 \times {r} \times \sin{(\frac{\theta}{2})} — where $\theta$ is the central angle in radians, and $r$ is the radius of the circle

## <a id="-practice-mcqs"></a>📝 Practice MCQs

**1. A circle has a radius of 21 cm. What is the area of the circle?**

A) A. 138.2 cm^2
B) B. 138.5 cm^2
C) C. 139 cm^2
D) D. 140 cm^2

**Answer:** B) The area of a circle is given by the formula A = πr^2. Substituting r = 21 cm, we get A = π(21)^2 = 138.5 cm^2.

---

**2. A semicircle has a diameter of 14 cm. What is its area?**

A) A. 77 cm^2
B) B. 78 cm^2
C) C. 79 cm^2
D) D. 80 cm^2

**Answer:** A) The area of a semicircle is half the area of a circle. Since the circle has a radius of 7 cm, its area is A = π(7)^2 = 153.94 cm^2. Hence, the area of the semicircle is 153.94/2 = 77 cm^2.

---

**3. A circle has a circumference of 88 cm. What is its radius?**

A) A. 14 cm
B) B. 14.2 cm
C) C. 14.5 cm
D) D. 15 cm

**Answer:** A) The circumference of a circle is given by C = 2πr. Substituting C = 88 cm, we get 88 = 2πr, which gives r = 14 cm.

---

**4. A sector of a circle has a central angle of 60° and an arc length of 16 cm. What is the radius of the circle?**

A) A. 10 cm
B) B. 10.5 cm
C) C. 11 cm
D) D. 12 cm

**Answer:** B) The arc length of a sector of a circle is given by s = θ/360 × 2πr, where θ is the central angle in degrees. Substituting s = 16 cm and θ = 60°, we get 16 = 60/360 × 2πr, which gives r = 10.5 cm.

---

**5. A circle has a radius of 18 cm. What is the length of its chord, which subtends an angle of 60° at the center?**

A) A. 15 cm
B) B. 16 cm
C) C. 17 cm
D) D. 18 cm

**Answer:** A) The length of a chord of a circle that subtends an angle θ at the center is given by l = 2r sin(θ/2). Substituting r = 18 cm and θ = 60°, we get l = 2(18) sin(30°) = 18 cm.
