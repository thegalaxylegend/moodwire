## <a id="-the-5-mistakes-that-cost-marks"></a>🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks

- **Mistake 1:** Incorrectly defining poverty line, which is the minimum amount of money needed to purchase a basket of goods and services necessary for survival.

- *Costs:* 2-3 marks
 

- *Fix:* Recall that poverty line is determined by the {$(PL = rac{VP}{Q})$} where {$(PL)$} is poverty line, {$(VP)$} is value of the commodities in the poverty line basket, and {$(Q)$} is quantity of the commodities in the poverty line basket.

- **Mistake 2:** Not differentiating between absolute poverty and relative poverty. Absolute poverty refers to a lack of basic necessities, while relative poverty is a measure that compares an individual's resources to those of others in the community.

- *Costs:* 4-5 marks
 

- *Fix:* Be aware that absolute poverty is often measured using a {$(POV = rac{P}{T})$} where {$(POV)$} is percentage of population below poverty line, {$(P)$} is number of people below poverty line, and {$(T)$} is total population.

- **Mistake 3:** Misunderstanding the concept of unemployment, which is a situation where people are willing and able to work but cannot find jobs.

- *Costs:* 3-4 marks
 

- *Fix:* Understand that unemployment can be calculated using the {$(UR = rac{U}{LF} 	imes 100)$} formula where {$(UR)$} is unemployment rate, {$(U)$} is number of unemployed people, and {$(LF)$} is labour force.

- **Mistake 4:** Not recognizing the different types of unemployment, including frictional, structural, cyclical, and seasonal.

- *Costs:* 4-5 marks
 

- *Fix:* Learn to identify each type of unemployment and their causes. For instance, frictional unemployment occurs due to workers voluntarily leaving jobs or being fired, and is calculated using the {$(F = rac{NRF}{LF})$} formula where {$(F)$} is frictional unemployment rate, {$(NRF)$} is number of people in the labour force not working, and {$(LF)$} is labour force.

- **Mistake 5:** Incorrectly applying the concept of human development index (HDI) which is a composite measure of life expectancy, education, and income.

- *Costs:* 5-6 marks
 

- *Fix:* Be aware of the {$(HDI = rac{LEI + EI + II}{3})$} formula where {$(HDI)$} is human development index, {$(LEI)$} is life expectancy index, {$(EI)$} is education index, and {$(II)$} is income index.

 - **Solution:**

 Relative poverty, on the other hand, refers to a lack of resources compared to others in the same society.

## <a id="-practice-mcqs"></a>📝 Practice MCQs

**1. If the demand for a commodity increases, what will happen to its price?**
**A)**   Its price will decrease
**B)**   Its price will increase
**C)**   Its price will remain the same
**D)**   There will be no effect on price

**Answer:** B) When demand for a commodity increases, the inverse relationship between demand and price dictates that the price will increase.

---

**2. Which of the following is an effect of poverty?**
**A)**   Increased savings
**B)**   Higher standard of living
**C)**   Lower unemployment rate
**D)**   Decreased standard of living

**Answer:** D) Poverty leads to reduced access to basic necessities, resulting in a decreased standard of living.

---

**3. What is the main cause of unemployment?**
**A)**   Lack of education
**B)**   Inadequate infrastructure
**C)**   High demand for labor
**D)**   Inadequate job opportunities

**Answer:** D) Unemployment arises primarily due to the mismatch between available job opportunities and the number of individuals seeking employment.

---

**4. Which of the following is a characteristic of underemployment?**
**A)**   Working more hours than needed
**B)**   Working fewer hours than desired
**C)**   Working for lower wages than expected
**D)**   Working for a job that does not utilize skills

**Answer:** B) Underemployment is characterized by individuals working fewer hours or having a lower productivity level than desired.

---

**5. What is the primary goal of a welfare program?**
**A)**   To increase GDP
**B)**   To redistribute income
**C)**   To reduce poverty
**D)**   To decrease unemployment

**Answer:** C) The primary objective of a welfare program is to alleviate poverty by providing financial assistance to those in need.
