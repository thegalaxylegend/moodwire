## ⚡ Formula Bank
- Poverty line is defined as the minimum level of income required to secure the basic necessities of life, which is usually $ \frac{2400}{\text{calories}} $ calories per person per day.
- Headcount Ratio (HCR) is the percentage of population living below the poverty line, given by $ \frac{\text{number of poor people}}{\text{total population}} \times 100 $.
- Poverty Gap Index (PGI) measures the depth of poverty, calculated as $ \frac{\text{poverty gap}}{\text{poverty line}} $.
- Gini Coefficient is a measure of income inequality, ranging from 0 (perfect equality) to 1 (perfect inequality), given by $ \frac{\text{area between Lorenz curve and line of equality}}{\text{total area}} $.
- Unemployment rate is the percentage of labor force that is currently unemployed, calculated as $ \frac{\text{number of unemployed people}}{\text{total labor force}} \times 100 $.
- Labor force participation rate is the percentage of population that is actively working or seeking work, given by $ \frac{\text{number of people in labor force}}{\text{total population}} \times 100 $.

- **Question 2:** What is the difference between unemployment and underemployment?
  - Unemployment refers to the state of being without a job, while underemployment refers to the state of being employed, but not fully utilized.
  - Underemployment can take many forms, including working part-time or in a job that does not utilize one's skills and qualifications.
- **Question 3:** What is the Gini Coefficient, and what does it measure?
  - The Gini Coefficient is a measure of income inequality, ranging from 0 (perfect equality) to 1 (perfect inequality).
  - It is calculated as $ \frac{\text{area between Lorenz curve and line of equality}}{\text{total area}} $, and it provides a snapshot of the distribution of income within a population.
