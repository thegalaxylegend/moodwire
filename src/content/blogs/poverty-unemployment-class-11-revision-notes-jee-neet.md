## ⚡ Formula Bank
- Poverty line is defined as the minimum level of income required to secure the basic necessities of life
- Headcount ratio = (number of people below poverty line)/(total population) × 100
- Poverty gap index = (average poverty gap)/(poverty line)
- Gini coefficient = (area between line of equality and Lorenz curve)/(total area under line of equality)
- Human poverty index (HPI) = (1/3) × (life expectancy index + literacy index + income index)
- Unemployment rate = (number of unemployed people)/(labour force) × 100
- Labour force participation rate = (labour force)/(total population) × 100
- Unemployment gap = (actual GDP - potential GDP)/(potential GDP)
- Okun's law states that a 1% increase ∈ unemployment is associated with a 2% decrease ∈ GDP

## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks

## 🧠 The One Thing Most Students Get Wrong
- Most students struggle to understand the concept of poverty and its measurement, often confusing absolute poverty with relative poverty
- Absolute poverty refers to a fixed standard of living, usually defined as a minimum level of income required to secure basic necessities
- Relative poverty, on the other hand, is a more subjective measure, comparing an individual's income to the median or average income ∈ a given area
- Understanding the distinction between these two concepts is crucial for accurately calculating poverty rates and developing effective poverty reduction strategies
