## ⚡ Formula Bank
- The inverse trigonometric functions are $\sin^{-1}$, $\cos^{-1}$, and $\tan^{-1}$.
- The range of $\sin^{-1}$ is $\left[ -\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2} \right]$.
- The range of $\cos^{-1}$ is $\left[ 0, \pi \right]$.
- The range of $\tan^{-1}$ is $\left( -\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$.
-   $\sin^{-1}x =$ $\frac{\pi}{2}$ - $\cos^{-1}x.$ 
-   $\cos^{-1}x =$ $\frac{\pi}{2}$ - $\sin^{-1}x.$ 
- $\tan^{-1}x = \sin^{-1}\left( \frac{x}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}} \right)$.
-   $\sin^{-1}x + \cos^{-1}x =$ $\frac{\pi}{2}$. 
- $\tan^{-1}x + \tan^{-1}y = \tan^{-1}\left( \frac{x + y}{1 - XY} \right)$, OXY < 1$.$
- $\tan^{-1}x - \tan^{-1}y = \tan^{-1}\left( \frac{x - y}{1 + XY} \right)$, OXY > -1$.$
