## 🧠 The One Thing Most Students Get Wrong
- Most students get confused between the product rule and the quotient rule for exponents
- The product rule states that aⁿ × aᵐ = aⁿ⁺ᵐ
- The quotient rule states that aⁿ ÷ aᵐ = aⁿ⁻ᵐ
- Make sure to use the correct rule for the given expression
