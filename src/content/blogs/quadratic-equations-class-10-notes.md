## ⚡ Formula Bank
- The general form of a quadratic equation is $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are constants and $a 
eq 0$.
- The roots of the quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ can be found using the quadratic formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.
- The discriminant of the quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ is given by $\delta = b^2 - 4ac$.
- If $\delta > 0$, the equation has two distinct real roots.
- If $\delta = 0$, the equation has two equal real roots.
- If $\delta < 0$, the equation has no real roots.
- The sum of the roots of the quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ is given by $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$.
- The product of the roots of the quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ is given by $\alpha \beta = \frac{c}{a}$.

## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks
- Not checking if $a = 0$ before applying the quadratic formula.
- Forgetting to consider the $\pm$ sign when finding the roots using the quadratic formula.
- Not simplifying the expression under the square root in the quadratic formula.
- Incorrectly calculating the sum and product of the roots.
- Not checking the discriminant to determine the nature of the roots.

## 📝 Practice MCQs
**1. What is the sum of the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 + 2x + 1 = 0$?**
-
A) 1
-
B) -2
-
C) 2
-
D) -1

**Answer: B) -2. Explanation: The sum of the roots is given by $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$, so $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{2}{1} = -2$.**

**2. What is the product of the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$?**
-
A) 12
-
B) -12
-
C) 7
-
D) -7

**Answer: A) 12. Explanation: The product of the roots is given by $\alpha \beta = \frac{c}{a}$, so $\alpha \beta = \frac{12}{1} = 12$.**

**3. What is the nature of the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 + 2x + 2 = 0$?**
-
A) Two distinct real roots
-
B) Two equal real roots
-
C) No real roots
-
D) One real root and one complex root

**Answer: C) No real roots. Explanation: The discriminant is given by $\delta = b^2 - 4ac = 2^2 - 4(1)(2) = 4 - 8 = -4 < 0$, so the equation has no real roots.**

**4. What are the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 - 9x + 20 = 0$?**
-
A) $x = 4$ or $x = 5$
-
B) $x = -4$ or $x = -5$
-
C) $x = 4$ or $x = -5$
-
D) $x = -4$ or $x = 5$

**Answer: A) $x = 4$ or $x = 5$. Explanation: The equation can be factorized as $(x - 4)(x - 5) = 0$, so $x = 4$ or $x = 5$.**

**5. What is the value of the discriminant of the quadratic equation $x^2 + 3x + 2 = 0$?**
-
A) 1
-
B) 5
-
C) 9
-
D) 1

**Answer: B) 5. Explanation: The discriminant is given by $\delta = b^2 - 4ac = 3^2 - 4(1)(2) = 9 - 8 = 1$.**
