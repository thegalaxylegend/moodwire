**1. What is the internal energy of an ideal gas?**

- B) ΔU = Q - W

**Answer: C) ΔU = nCvΔT. Internal energy of an ideal gas is given by the formula ΔU = nCvΔT.**

**2. What is the speed of sound ∈ a gas?**
- A) v = √(γRT/M)
- B) v = √(γP/ρ)
- C) v = √(RT/M)
- D) v = √(P/ρ)
**Answer: A) v = √(γRT/M). Speed of sound ∈ a gas is given by the formula v = √(γRT/M).**
