## ✏️ 3 Solved PYQs
- **Question 1:** A block of mass 2 kg is heated from 20°C to 50°C. If the specific heat capacity of the block is 200 J/kg°C, find the energy transferred to the block.
- Step 1: Identify the given quantities - mass (m) = 2 kg, initial temperature (T1) = 20°C, final temperature (T2) = 50°C, specific heat capacity (c) = 200 J/kg°C
- Step 2: Use the formula Q = mcΔT to find the energy transferred
- Step 3: Plug in the values to get Q = 2 kg × 200 J/kg°C × (50°C - 20°C) = 2 kg × 200 J/kg°C × 30°C = 12000 J
- **Answer:** 12000 J
- **Question 2:** A lens has a focal length of 20 cm. If an object is placed 30 cm from the lens, find the image distance.
- Step 1: Identify the given quantities - focal length (f) = 20 cm, object distance (u) = -30 cm
- Step 2: Use the formula 1/f = 1/v + 1/u to find the image distance
- Step 3: Plug in the values to get 1/20 = 1/v - 1/30
- Step 4: Solve for v to get v = 60 cm
- **Answer:** 60 cm
- **Question 3:** A gas is heated from 20°C to 50°C. If the initial pressure is 1 atm, find the final pressure.
- Step 1: Identify the given quantities - initial temperature (T1) = 20°C, final temperature (T2) = 50°C, initial pressure (P1) = 1 atm
- Step 2: Use the formula P1/T1 = P2/T2 to find the final pressure
- Step 3: Plug in the values to get 1 atm / 293 K = P2 / 323 K
- Step 4: Solve for P2 to get P2 = 1.1 atm
- **Answer:** 1.1 atm
