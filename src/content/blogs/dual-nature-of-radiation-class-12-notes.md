The significance of the photoelectric effect in demonstrating the dual nature of radiation can be understood through several key observations and experiments. This threshold frequency is related to the work function of the metal, which is the minimum energy needed to remove an electron from the metal surface.

a prism

a concave lens

is refracted through a prism

λ = m * v
λ = 1 / m

**Answer:** A) The de Broglie wavelength of a particle is given by the equation λ = h / p, where h is Planck's constant and p is the momentum of the particle.

light wave passing through a prism
electron beam passing through a double-slit
sound wave passing through a hollow pipe
X-ray beam passing through a crystal lattice

**Answer:** B) The electron beam passing through a double-slit is an example of wave-particle duality, demonstrating the wave-like behavior of particles such as electrons.

classical physics
quantum mechanics
relativity
wave-particle duality
