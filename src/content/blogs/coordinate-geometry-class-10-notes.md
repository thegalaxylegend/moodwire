## 🧠 The One Thing Most Students Get Wrong
- The most common mistake students make is not checking if the line is parallel to the x-axis or y-axis when finding the equation of a line.
- When the line is parallel to the x-axis, the slope is 0, and the equation of the line is of the form by = CD.
- When the line is parallel to the y-axis, the slope is undefined, and the equation of the line is of the form $x$ = CD.
- Students often forget to consider these special cases and end up with incorrect equations for the lines.

## 👁️ Ayush's Note
- To avoid mistakes, always check the slope of the line before finding its equation.
- If the slope is 0, the line is parallel to the x-axis, and if the slope is undefined, the line is parallel to the y-axis.
- Make sure to use the correct formula for the equation of a circle and simplify it to its standard form.
- Always double-check your calculations and consider all possible cases to avoid errors.
