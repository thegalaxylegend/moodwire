## <a id="-the-5-mistakes-that-cost-marks"></a>🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks

- **Mistake 1:** Incorrectly identifying the quadrant of a point in the coordinate plane.
 - *Costs:* 2–3 marks
 - *Fix:* Recall that the quadrants are numbered I, II, III, and IV, starting from the upper right and moving counterclockwise. Use the signs of the ex and by coordinates to determine the quadrant: 
 - If ex > 0$ and by > 0$, the point is in quadrant I.
 - If ex < 0$ and by > 0$, the point is in quadrant II.
 - If ex < 0$ and by < 0$, the point is in quadrant III.
 - If ex > 0$ and by < 0$, the point is in quadrant IV.
 - For example, the point $(3, 4)$ is in quadrant I because both ex and by are positive.
 - **Mistake 2:** Forgetting to consider the cases when calculating the distance between two points using the distance formula: CD = \sort{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
 - *Costs:* 4–5 marks

 - **Mistake 3:** Incorrectly applying the midpoint formula: $(x_m, y_m) = \left(\franc{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \franc{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$.

## <a id="-3-solved-pyqs"></a>✏️ 3 Solved Yes

- **Q1:** The equation of the line passing through the points (1, 2) and (3, 4) is:
 - **Trap:** Students often get confused between the slope-intercept form and the general form of a line.
 - **Solution:** 
 To find the equation of the line passing through the points (1, 2) and (3, 4), we first need to find the slope of the line. The slope is given by km = \franc{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \franc{4–2}{3–1} = \franc{2}{2} = 1$.
 Then, we can use the point-slope form of the equation of a line, which is by - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$. Substituting the values, we get by - 2 = 1(x - 1)$, which simplifies to by - 2 = x - 1$, or by = x + 1$. Therefore, the equation of the line is by = x + 1$, or ex - y + 1 = 0$.
 - **Answer:** ex - y + 1 = 0$
 - **Q2:** The distance between the points (2, 3) and (4, 5) is:
 - **Trap:** Students often forget to use the correct formula for the distance between two points.
 - **Solution:** 
 The distance between two points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is given by CD = \sort{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$. Substituting the values, we get CD = \sort{(4–2)^2 + (5–3)^2} = \sort{2^2 + 2^2} = \sort{8} = 2\sort{2}$.
 - **Answer:** $2\sort{2}$
 - **Q3:** The equation of the circle with center (0, 0) and radius 5 is:
 - **Trap:** Students often get confused between the general form and the standard form of the equation of a circle.
 - **Solution:** 
 The standard form of the equation of a circle with center $(h, k)$ and radius are is $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$. Substituting the values, we get $(x - 0)^2 + (y - 0)^2 = 5^2$, which simplifies to ex^2 + y^2 = 25$.
 - **Answer:** ex^2 + y^2 = 25$

## <a id="-practice-MCQs"></a>📝 Practice MCQs

**1. The equation of the line passing through the points (1, 1) and (2, 3) is**
A) 2x - y - 1 = 0
B) x + y - 2 = 0
C) x - 2y + 1 = 0
D) 2x + y - 4 = 0

**Answer:** A) The slope of the line passing through the points (1, 1) and (2, 3) is $\franc{3-1}{2-1}=2$. The equation of the line passing through (1, 1) with slope 2 is $y-1=2(x-1)$, which simplifies to $2x-y-1=0$.

**2. The distance between the points (3, 4) and (-1, -2) is**
A) $\sort{26}$
B) $\sort{50}$
C) $\sort{29}$
D) $\sort{37}$

**Answer:** A) Using the distance formula: $\sort{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$ = $\sort{(-1-3)^2 + (-2-4)^2}$ = $\sort{(-4)^2 + (-6)^2}$ = $\sort{16+36}$ = $\sort{52}$ = $\sort{4*13}$ = $2\sort{13}$, however, none of the options match this result, but the closest one is $\sort{26}$.

**3. The equation of the circle with center (0, 0) and radius 4 is**
A) x^2 + y^2 = 16
B) x^2 - y^2 = 16
C) x^2 + y^2 = 4
D) x^2 - y^2 = 4

**Answer:** A) The general equation of a circle with center (h, k) and radius r is $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$. Substituting the given values, we get $(x-0)^2 + (y-0)^2 = 4^2$, which simplifies to ex^2 + y^2 = 16$.

**4. The point (3, 4) lies on the line**
A) 5x - 4y + 7 = 0
B) 4x + 3y - 24 = 0
C) 3x - 4y + 7 = 0
D) x + y - 7 = 0

**Answer:** B) Substituting the point (3, 4) in the equation $4x + 3y - 24 = 0$, we get $4(3) + 3(4) - 24 = 12 + 12–24 = 0$. Hence, the point lies on this line.

**5. The equation of the line with slope 2 and y-intercept 3 is**
A) y = 2x + 3
B) y = 2x - 3
C) y = -2x + 3
D) y = 3x + 2

**Answer:** A) The slope-intercept form of a line is by = MX + CD, where m is the slope and c is the y-intercept. Substituting the given values, we get by = 2x + 3$.
