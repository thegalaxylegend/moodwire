## Quick Revision & Recap: Ancient India - Mahajanapadas
### Quick Recall Box
The **Mahajanapadas** were 16 powerful kingdoms that emerged in ancient India during the 6th century BCE. These kingdoms played a significant role in shaping the history of India. The following are the key points to recall:
- **Number of Mahajanapadas**: 16
- **Time Period**: 6th century BCE to 4th century BCE
- **Notable Mahajanapadas**: Magadha, Kosala, Kasi, Vrijji, Malla, Chedi, Vatsa, Kaushambi, Panchala, Matsya, Kurukshetra, Gandhara, Kamboja, Shakya, Lichchhavi, and Avanti

### Key Features of Mahajanapadas
- **Republics and Monarchies**: Some Mahajanapadas, like the Vrijji and Lichchhavi, were republics, while others, such as Magadha, were monarchies.
- **Economic Developments**: The period saw significant economic growth, with the development of **irrigation systems**, **trade**, and **crafts**.
- **Religious Movements**: The rise of Buddhism and Jainism, which challenged the traditional Vedic religion, was a hallmark of this period.
