**2. A body of mass 5 kg is projected from the origin at an angle of 60° with the horizontal. The initial velocity of the body is 20 m/s. The total energy of the body is**
**A)**   1000 J
**B)**   500 J
**C)**   2000 J
**D)**   1500 J

**Answer:** C) The initial velocity is 20 m/s. The angle of projection is 60°. The horizontal component of the velocity is 20 cos 60° = 10 m/s. The vertical component of the velocity is 20 sin 60° = 17.32 m/s. The potential energy of the body at the highest point is 0. The kinetic energy of the body at the highest point is (1/2)mv^2 = (1/2)*5*17.32^2 = 752.4 J. The kinetic energy of the body at the highest point is (1/2)mv^2 = (1/2)*5*10^2 = 250 J. The total energy of the body is the \sum of the potential energy and the kinetic energy at the highest point, which is 752.4 J + 250 J = 1002.4 J ≈ 1000 J.
