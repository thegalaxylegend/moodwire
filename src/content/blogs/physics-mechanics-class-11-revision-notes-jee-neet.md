## What is Physics: Mechanics?

Physics: Mechanics is the study of the motion of objects. It includes kinematics, dynamics, and energy. For Class 11 exam prep in 2026, the most important aspect is understanding the concepts of motion and forces.


| Concept | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Kinematics | Study of motion without considering forces |
| Dynamics | Study of motion considering forces |
| Energy | Ability to do work |


## What are the key concepts in Physics: Mechanics?

The key concepts in Physics: Mechanics include motion, forces, energy, and momentum.


| Concept | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Motion | Change in position of an object |
| Forces | Push or pull that causes motion |
| Energy | Ability to do work |
| Momentum | Product of mass and velocity |


## Quick Recall Box
