## ⚡ Formula Bank
- $v = u + at$ for uniformly accelerated motion
- $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$ for displacement under uniform acceleration
- $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ for final velocity after displacement under uniform acceleration
- $F = \frac{dp}{dt}$ for force in terms of momentum
- $F = ma$ for force in terms of mass and acceleration
- $p = mv$ for momentum
- $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$ for kinetic energy
- $U = mgh$ for potential energy
- $W = F \cdot s$ for work done
- $P = \frac{W}{t}$ for power
- $\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p}$ for angular momentum
- $\tau = r \times F$ for torque
- $I = \frac{m}{12}(a^2 + b^2)$ for moment of inertia of a rod
- $I = \frac{2}{5}mr^2$ for moment of inertia of a solid sphere
- $\omega = \frac{v}{r}$ for angular velocity
- $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$ for time period of a simple pendulum
- $T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$ for time period of a rotating body
- $f = \frac{1}{T}$ for frequency
- $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$ for centripetal acceleration
- $a_c = \omega^2r$ for centripetal acceleration in terms of angular velocity

## 🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks
- Not using the correct formula for a given situation
- Forgetting to convert units before plugging in values
- Not considering the sign of the quantity, especially in vector equations
- Not checking the units of the final answer
- Not using the correct equation for the specific type of motion or system being described

## ✏️ 3 Solved PYQs
- **Question 1:** A block of mass 2 kg is moving with a velocity of 3 m/s. Find its kinetic energy.
- Step 1: Identify the given values - $m = 2 kg$, $v = 3 m/s$
- Step 2: Use the formula $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$ to find the kinetic energy
- Step 3: Plug in the values - $K = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 3^2 = 9 J$
- **Answer:** 9 J
- **Question 2:** A car is moving in a circular path of radius 10 m. If its speed is 5 m/s, find its centripetal acceleration.
- Step 1: Identify the given values - $r = 10 m$, $v = 5 m/s$
- Step 2: Use the formula $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$ to find the centripetal acceleration
- Step 3: Plug in the values - $a_c = \frac{5^2}{10} = 2.5 m/s^2$
- **Answer:** 2.5 m/s^2
- **Question 3:** A simple pendulum has a length of 1 m. Find its time period.
- Step 1: Identify the given value - $l = 1 m$
- Step 2: Use the formula $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$ to find the time period
- Step 3: Plug in the values - $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{1}{10}} = 2\pi \times 0.316 = 1.98 s$
- **Answer:** 1.98 s

## 🧠 The One Thing Most Students Get Wrong
- Most students struggle with understanding the concept of relative motion and how to apply it to solve problems.
- The key is to understand that the motion of an object is relative to the observer, and to use the correct equations to describe the motion.
- For example, when two objects are moving relative to each other, the velocity of one object with respect to the other is the sum or difference of their individual velocities, depending on the direction of motion.
