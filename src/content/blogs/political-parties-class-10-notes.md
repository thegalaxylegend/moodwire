 *Let's execute.*### ⚡ Formula Bank: Political Parties

**The Compositional Framework**

- **Political Party Structure:** Leaders + Active Members + Followers — A party is not just a group of people; it must have a hierarchy of decision-makers, dedicated workers, and a mass base of supporters.

- **Party Component 1 (Leadership):** Decision-[making](/blog/the-making-of-a-global-world-class-10-notes) body + Policy formulation — The core group that decides the party's direction.

- **Party Component 2 (Active Members):** Grassroots workers + Organizational maintenance — Those who execute party tasks and manage local units.

- **Party Component 3 (Followers):** Voters + Mass support base — The people who vote for the party but do not necessarily participate in day-to-day operations.

**Examiner's Trap:** Students often assume "Followers" are the same as "Active Members." They are not. Members work; followers vote.

**The Functional Logic**

- **Core Function of Parties:** Contest Elections + Formulate Policies + Make Laws + Form Government + Play Opposition + Shape Public Opinion.

- **Legislative Function:** Policy Proposal + Parliamentary Debate + Voting + Law Implementation — The process by which a party's manifesto becomes a statute.

- **The Opposition Formula:** Scrutiny of Govt + Critique of Policies + Presentation of Alternative Views — The primary role of the party that is not in power.

- **Government Formation Logic:** Winning Majority (Seats > 50% of House) = Right to form Executive.

- **Public Opinion Shaping:** Party Manifesto + Media Presence + Mass Mobilization = Influence on Voter Behavior.

**Examiner's Trap:** Do not confuse "making laws" with "passing laws." Parties *propose* and *vote* on laws; the legislature passes them.

**The Classification Matrix (Crucial for MCQ/Case Studies)**

- **National Party Requirement A (Vote %):** ≥ 6% votes in Lok Sabha OR Assembly elections in 4 states.

- **National Party Requirement B (Seat Count):** ≥ 4 seats in the Lok Sabha.

- **National Party Status Formula:** (Requirement A) AND (Requirement B) — Both conditions must be met simultaneously.

- **State Party Requirement A (Vote %):** ≥ 6% votes in the State Legislative Assembly election.

- **State Party Requirement B (Seat Count):** ≥ 2 seats in the State Legislative Assembly.

- **State Party Status Formula:** (Requirement A) AND (Requirement B) — Both conditions must be met simultaneously.

**Examiner's Trap:** The most common error is forgetting the "AND" condition. A party with 10% votes, but 0 seats is NOT a National Party.

**The Party System Equations**

- **One-Party System:** 1 Dominant Party + Limited/No Competition = Non-democratic (e.g., China).

- **Two-Party System:** 2 Major Parties + Power Rotation = Competitive (e.g., USA, UK).

- **Multi-Party System:** > 2 Parties + Ideological Diversity + Coalition Politics = Complex (e.g., India).

- **Coalition Government Formula:** Party A + Party B + Party C (Total Seats > 50% of House) = Coalition Govt.

- **Coalition Logic:** No single party has a majority → Multiple parties join to reach the 50% + 1 threshold.

**Examiner's Trap:** Students often think a Multi-Party system *always* results in a coalition. A multi-party system exists if there are many parties, but a coalition only happens if no one wins a majority.

**The Challenge & Reform Logic**

- **Internal Democracy Deficit:** Power Concentration + Lack of Regular Internal Elections + Lack of Membership Access.

- **Dynastic Succession Formula:** Family Control + Merit Exclusion = Lack of Internal Democracy.

- **Money Power Impact:** High Election Spending + Influence on Candidate Selection = Inequality in Competition.

- **Muscle Power Impact:** Criminalization of Politics + Intimidation of Voters = Breakdown of Rule of Law.

- **Lack of Meaningful Choice:** Similarity in Policies + Overlapping Ideologies = Reduced Voter Agency.

- **Transparency Reform (Legal):** Filing of Affidavits + Disclosure of Assets + Disclosure of Criminal Cases.

- **Accountability Reform (Organizational):** Regular Internal Elections + Mandatory Membership Registers.

- **Anti-Defection Logic (52nd Amendment):** Member changes party after election → Automatic loss of seat.

**Examiner's Trap:** In the "Money Power" question, distinguish between "spending on campaigns" (legal but high) and "buying votes" (illegal).

**The Institutional Framework**

- **Election Commission Role:** Symbol Allotment + Code of Conduct Enforcement + Election Oversight.

- **Code of Conduct Rule:** No use of Govt [resources](/blog/resources-and-development-class-10-notes) + No announcement of new schemes after election dates + No communal appeals.

- **Party Manifesto:** Core Ideology + Policy Promises + Election Platform.

- **Mistake 1 — The Function-Challenge Identity Crisis:**

- 💸 **Marks lost:** 2 marks.
