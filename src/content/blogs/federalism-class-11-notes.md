## What is Federalism?
Federalism is a system of government in which power is divided between a central authority and constituent political units, such as states or provinces. It includes a division of powers, a bicameral legislature, and an independent judiciary. For Class 11 exam prep in 2026, the most important aspect is understanding the principles of federalism and its application in the Indian Constitution.

## What are the Key Features of Federalism in India?
In India, federalism has the following key features:
* **Bicameral Legislature**: The Parliament consists of two houses: the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha.
* **Independent Judiciary**: The judiciary is independent and has the power to interpret the Constitution.
* **System of Checks and Balances**: The executive, legislature, and judiciary have checks and balances to prevent abuse of power.

## Trap Exceptions
Be aware of the following trap exceptions:
* **Unitary Federalism**: A system where the central government has absolute power, and the states have limited autonomy.
* **Asymmetric Federalism**: A system where some states have more powers than others, leading to unequal distribution of power.
* **Confederalism**: A system where the states have more power than the central government, leading to a loose alliance.

Contemporary issues in federalism include:
* **Reorganization of States**: The reorganization of states has led to the creation of new states, raising questions about the distribution of power and resources.
* **Center-State Relations**: The relationship between the central government and the states is crucial, with issues such as financial devolution, law and order, and policy implementation.

## What are the Future Directions of Federalism?
The future directions of federalism include:
* **Cooperative Federalism**: A system where the central government and the states work together to achieve common goals and address national challenges.
* **Competitive Federalism**: A system where states compete with each other to attract investment, talent, and resources, leading to economic growth and development.
* **Inclusive Federalism**: A system where the rights and interests of all citizens, including marginalized communities, are protected and promoted.
