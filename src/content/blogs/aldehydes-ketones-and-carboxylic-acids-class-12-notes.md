## ⚡ Formula Bank
- Aldehydes and ketones are functional group isomers, with the general formula $\mathrm{RCHO}$ for aldehydes and $\mathrm{RCOR'}$ for ketones.
- Carboxylic acids have the general formula $\mathrm{RCOOH}$, with a carbonyl group $\mathrm{C=O}$ and a hydroxyl group $\mathrm{OH}$.
- The acidity of carboxylic acids can be represented by the equation $\mathrm{RCOOH} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{RCOO^-} + \mathrm{H^+}$, with an acid dissociation constant $K_a = \frac{[\mathrm{RCOO^-}][\mathrm{H^+}]}{[\mathrm{RCOOH}]}$.
- Aldehydes and ketones can undergo nucleophilic addition reactions, with the general equation $\mathrm{RCHO} + \mathrm{Nu^-} \rightarrow \mathrm{RCHONu}$.
- The Wolff-Kishner reduction of aldehydes and ketones can be represented by the equation $\mathrm{RCHO} + \mathrm{NH_2NH_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{RCH_3} + \mathrm{N_2}$.
- The aldol condensation reaction can be represented by the equation $\mathrm{RCHO} + \mathrm{R'CHO} \rightarrow \mathrm{RCH=CHR'} + \mathrm{H_2O}$.
