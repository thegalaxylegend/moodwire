## <a id="-what-will-come-in-your-exam"></a>🎯 What WILL Come in Your Exam
* 1-2 numericals on Wolff-Kishner reduction — always, involving $${C_6H_5COCH_3 + H_2N-NH_2 \rightarrow C_6H_5CH_3 + H_2O + N_2}$$
* Preparation of aldehydes and ketones from alkyl halides, acid chlorides, and carboxylic acids — high-yield, expect 1-2 questions
* Reactions of aldehydes and ketones with $${NH_2OH}$$, $${NH_2NH_2}$$, and $${H_2N-NH_2}$$ — always, including $${\\Delta}$$G and yields
* Aldol condensation and its mechanism — favourite, with at least 1 question involving $${\\alpha, \\beta}$$-unsaturated compounds
* Cannizzaro reaction — guaranteed, with a focus on $${\\frac{1}{2}}$${R_2CO}$$ + $${\\frac{1}{2}}$${R_2CO}$$ $${\\rightarrow}$$ $${R_2CH_2OH}$$ + $${R_2CO_3}$$
* Haloform reaction of methyl ketones — JEE/NEET favourite, using $${\\times}$$ $${Cl_2}$$, $${Br_2}$$, or $${I_2}$$
* Reduction of carboxylic acids to aldehydes using $${LiAlH_4}$$ or $${NaBH_4}$$ — high-yield, 1-2 questions
* Carboxylic acid reactions with $${SOCl_2}$$, $${P_2O_5}$$, and $${P_4O_10}$$ — always, including the formation of acid anhydrides and acyl chlorides
* Hydrolysis and esterification of carboxylic acids — guaranteed, with a focus on $${K_a}$$, $${K_b}$$, and $${pH}$$ dependence
* Ring chain tautomerism — JEE/NEET favourite, involving $${\\Delta}$$G and equilibrium constants
* Acidity of $${\\alpha}$$-hydrogen atoms in aldehydes and ketones — high-yield, with at least 1 question involving $${pKa}$$ values
* Spectroscopy (IR, NMR) of aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids — always, with a focus on peak assignments and structural elucidation
* Stereochemistry of aldol condensation and related reactions — favourite, involving $${R/S}$$ configurations and $${E/Z}$$ isomerism

 $RCHO + NH_3 + H_2O \xrightarrow{OH^-} RCONH_2 + H_2$ : Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis, $R$ = alkyl group \
 $RCOCH_3 + NaOH \rightarrow RCOO^-Na^+ + CH_3OH$ : Hydrolysis of Ketones, $R$ = alkyl group \
 $RCHO + CH_3MgBr \rightarrow RCH(OH)CH_3$ : Grignard Reaction with Aldehydes, $R$ = alkyl group \
 $R_2C=O + CH_3MgBr \rightarrow R_2C(OH)CH_3$ : Grignard Reaction with Ketones, $R$ = alkyl group \
