## MCQs


**1. What is the primary mode of heat transfer in solids?**
a) Convection
b) Conduction
c) Radiation
d) Diffusion

**Answer:** b) Conduction is the primary mode of heat transfer in solids, where heat is transferred through direct contact between particles.


**2. Which of the following is an example of reflection of light?**
a) Refraction of light through a prism
b) Diffraction of light around an obstacle
c) Reflection of light off a mirror
d) Total internal reflection of light

**Answer:** c) Reflection of light off a mirror is an example of reflection, where the light bounces back from the surface.
