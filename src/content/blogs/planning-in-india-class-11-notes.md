## How does Planning work in India?

Planning in India works through the Five-Year Plans, which are formulated by the Planning Commission of India. The plans are implemented by the central and state governments, and the progress is monitored by the Planning Commission.

## MCQs


**1. Which of the following is the main objective of planning in India?**
a) Rapid industrialization
b) Growth with social justice
c) Removal of poverty
d) Increase in national income

**Answer:** b) Growth with social justice is the main objective of planning in India, as it aims to promote economic growth while ensuring social welfare.


**2. Which plan is known for its focus on rapid industrialization?**
a) First Five-Year Plan
b) Second Five-Year Plan
c) Third Five-Year Plan
d) Fourth Five-Year Plan

**Answer:** b) The Second Five-Year Plan is known for its focus on rapid industrialization, as it aimed to promote rapid economic growth through industrial development.
