## <a id="-practice-mcqs"></a>📝 Practice MCQs

**1. The primary function of a local government is to?**
**A)**   to make laws for the nation
**B)**   to manage the country's finances
**C)**   to provide basic services to the citizens
**D)**   to maintain law and order in the nation

**Answer:** D) to maintain law and order in the nation

---

**2. Who are the key stakeholders in local governance?**
**A)**   citizens, government officials, and politicians
**B)**   government officials, politicians, and NGOs
**C)**   citizens, NGOs, and private sector entities
**D)**   government officials, politicians, and private sector entities

**Answer:** A) citizens, government officials, and politicians

---

**3. What is the significance of local self-government?**
**A)**   it allows the government to make decisions without consulting citizens
**B)**   it enables citizens to participate in decision-making processes
**C)**   it is a way for the government to centralize power
**D)**   it allows the government to allocate resources efficiently

**Answer:** B) it enables citizens to participate in decision-making processes

---

**4. What is the primary goal of decentralization in local government?**
**A)**   to reduce the power of the central government
**B)**   to increase the role of NGOs in governance
**C)**   to increase citizen participation in decision-making
**D)**   to reduce the financial burden on local governments

**Answer:** C) to increase citizen participation in decision-making

---

**5. What is the importance of local government elections?**
**A)**   they allow citizens to choose their representatives
**B)**   they are not necessary for effective governance
**C)**   they are a way for the government to centralize power
**D)**   they are a formality with no real significance

**Answer:** A) they allow citizens to choose their representatives
