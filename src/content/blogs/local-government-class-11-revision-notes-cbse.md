## <a id="-the-5-mistakes-that-cost-marks"></a>🪤 The 5 Mistakes That Cost Marks

- *Fix:* Read and revise the key features of these amendment acts, such as the establishment of three-tier Panchayati Raj institutions in rural areas and municipalities in urban areas, and understand how they have impacted local governance.

- **Mistake 2:** Confusing the roles and responsibilities of the Gram Sabha and the Gram Panchayat.

- *Costs:* 3-5 marks, particularly in questions that require distinguishing between these two entities.

- *Fix:* Clearly understand that the Gram Sabha is a body of persons registered in the electoral rolls within the area covered under a Gram Panchayat, while the Gram Panchayat is the executive committee, and know their respective roles in local governance.

- **Mistake 3:** Not being able to differentiate between a Municipal Corporation and a Municipal Council.

- *Costs:* 4-6 marks, especially in questions comparing urban local governments.

- *Fix:* Memorize the key differences, such as the population criteria (e.g.

- *Costs:* 3-5 marks, particularly in questions related to the conduct of elections for local bodies.

- *Fix:* Study the role of the State Election Commission in supervising, directing, and controlling the preparation of electoral rolls and the conduct of elections to the local governments.

- **Mistake 5:** Not understanding the concept of {${{ reservation}}$} in local governments, including the provision for {${{SC}}$}, {${{ST}}$}, and {${{OBC}}$} communities, as well as women.

- *Costs:* 5-7 marks, especially in questions related to social justice and representation in local governance.

## <a id="-3-solved-pyqs"></a>✏️ 3 Solved PYQs

- **Q1:** What is the main difference between a Municipal Corporation and a Municipal Council in the context of local government in India?
 - **Trap:** Students often get confused between the roles and responsibilities of Municipal Corporations and Municipal Councils.
 - **Solution:** 
 - Municipal Corporations are typically found in larger cities and have a more complex structure, with a Mayor and a Commissioner.
 - Municipal Councils, on the other hand, are found in smaller cities and towns and have a simpler structure, with a President and a Secretary.
 - The main difference between the two lies in their size, structure, and the services they provide to the citizens.
 - **Answer:** Municipal Corporations have a more complex structure and provide a wider range of services to citizens.
 - **Q2:** What are the powers and functions of the Gram Sabha in the context of local government in India?
 - **Trap:** Students often struggle to identify the specific powers and functions of the Gram Sabha.
 - **Solution:** 
 - The Gram Sabha has the power to approve the annual budget of the Gram Panchayat.
 - It also has the power to review the work of the Gram Panchayat and make recommendations for improvement.
 - The Gram Sabha can also identify beneficiaries for various government schemes and programs.
 - **Answer:** The Gram Sabha has the power to approve the annual budget, review the work of the Gram Panchayat, and identify beneficiaries for government schemes.
 - **Q3:** What is the significance of the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments in the context of local government in India?
 - **Trap:** Students often find it difficult to understand the impact of these amendments on local government.
 - **Solution:** 
 - The 73rd Amendment established the three-tier system of local government, comprising the Gram Panchayat, Mandal Panchayat, and Zilla Panchayat.
 - The 74th Amendment established the three-tier system of urban local government, comprising the Municipal Corporation, Municipal Council, and Nagar Panchayat.
 - These amendments aimed to strengthen local government and increase citizen participation in governance.
 - **Answer:** The 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments established the three-tier system of local government and aimed to strengthen local government and increase citizen participation.

**1. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a local government?**
**A)**   It has its own budget
**B)**   It has the power to make laws
**C)**   It has a limited jurisdiction
**D)**   It is directly elected by the people

**Answer:** C) A local government has a limited jurisdiction but not limited to the ones mentioned in the options.

---

**2. What is the primary function of a local government in India?**
**A)**   To make national laws
**B)**   To collect and distribute taxes
**C)**   To provide civic amenities and services
**D)**   To handle foreign policy

**Answer:** C) Local governments provide civic amenities and services like water supply, sanitation, and waste management.

---

**3. What is the term for the division of powers between the central and state governments in India?**
**A)**   Delegated powers
**B)**   Concurrent powers
**C)**   Residual powers
**D)**   Cooperative federalism

**Answer:** C) Residual powers refer to the powers that are not explicitly mentioned in the Constitution.

**4. Which of the following is an example of a local government in India?**
**A)**   Panchayat
**B)**   Municipal Corporation
**C)**   State Legislature
**D)**   National Parliament

**Answer:** A) Panchayats are local governments in rural areas, while Municipal Corporations are local governments in urban areas.
