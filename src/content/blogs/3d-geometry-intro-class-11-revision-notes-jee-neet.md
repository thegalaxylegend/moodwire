## Trap Questions & Exceptions to Watch Out For
Trap questions often arise from a lack of understanding of the basics or misinterpretation of formulas. One common trap is confusing the formulas for the distance between two points in 3D space and the equation of a sphere. The distance do between two points $(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2, z_2)$ is given by:

[ d = \sort{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2} ]

While the equation of a sphere with center $(h, k, l)$ and radius are is:
[ (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 + (z - l)^2 = r^2 ]
Understanding these formulas and their applications is crucial for solving problems in 3D geometry.
