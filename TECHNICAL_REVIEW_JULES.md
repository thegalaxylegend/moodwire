# 🧭 Technical Review: Exam Compass (Project "Nexus")

Exam Compass is a high-performance, AI-maintained EdTech platform designed for competitive exam preparation (JEE, NEET, CUET, and Boards). It distinguishes itself through a "Self-Healing" content ecosystem and an aggressive SEO strategy.

---

## 🏗️ 1. Technical Architecture
The platform leverages a cutting-edge **React 19 + Vite** stack, optimized for the "Instant Web" experience.

*   **Hybrid SSG/SPA Approach:** Unlike traditional SPAs, Exam Compass uses a custom Node.js prerendering pipeline (`scripts/prerender-all.js`). It generates static HTML for over 2,000+ question and blog pages, ensuring **0.5s LCP** and near-instant time-to-interactive.
*   **Zero-Latency Shell:** The site serves a "boot-shell" skeleton that hydrates into a full SPA, providing the speed of static sites with the interactivity of a modern app.
*   **Manual Chunking Strategy:** In `vite.config.ts`, the developer has manually split vendor libraries (Framer Motion, Lucide, UI components) to prevent "mega-bundle" bloat, ensuring high Lighthouse scores.

## 🤖 2. The "Jules" AI Ecosystem
The most impressive feature is the **Jules Maintenance Pipeline**, a suite of 100+ automation scripts that manage the site's content health:

*   **Self-Healing Content:** Scripts like `deep-latex-repair.mjs` and `sanity-guard.ts` automatically scan and fix broken mathematical formulas (LaTeX) and dead links.
*   **Automated Content Generation:** The `blog-generator.ts` and `topic-researcher.mjs` agents can research competitive exam trends and produce SEO-optimized study materials without human intervention.
*   **Question Verification:** A dedicated pipeline (`verify-question-db.js`) ensures the integrity of the question bank, checking for duplicate IDs and malformed metadata.

## 📈 4. Technical SEO & Growth
Exam Compass is built for organic search dominance:
*   **Programmatic SEO:** Automated generation of `seo-manifest.json` creates thousands of landing pages for specific exam topics (e.g., `/jee-main/physics/electrostatics`).
*   **Rich Snippet Mastery:** The platform automatically generates and injects **JSON-LD schemas** (FAQPage, Quiz, and Article) into static HTML, making the site highly likely to appear in Google's "People Also Ask" and Rich Result boxes.
